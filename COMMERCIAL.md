# Commercial use

Everything in this repository is free for non-commercial use: build a radio for your own family, study it, change it and share your changes.

Commercial use needs a licence from the copyright holder. That includes:

- building radios to sell, or to supply as part of a paid service
- selling kits, cases or assembled units based on these designs
- using the code, documents or images in a commercial product

## How to ask

Open an issue on this repository titled "Commercial licence", or contact Chris Barlow through [GitHub](https://github.com/cgbarlow). Say what you would like to make and roughly how many.
