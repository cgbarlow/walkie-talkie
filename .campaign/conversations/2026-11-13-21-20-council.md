**🐻 Bear:**

**🐱 Cat:**

1. **Open channel breaks a published promise.** The R1 doc says "Mic live only while the button is held, never a remote listen-in." Prototypes A and B say the microphone is "off by default ... and there never will be." Add open mode without changing that copy and the site is wrong. Open mode needs hard fences: switched on at the unit only, never from afar; a steady red light whenever the mic can hear; spoken "channel open" and "channel closed"; the other end can see it is open; Grandad agrees to it.
2. **The audio design assumes half duplex.** Your ESP32 doc skips echo cancelling because only one person talks at a time, and says it would cost 820 KB and a third of a core. If both ends are open, the speaker feeds the mic. The safe first version is voice-activated, one at a time, with the mic muted while the speaker plays. Phone-style two-way is a later, heavier job. The relay also ends a turn after 1.5 s of silence or 60 s total; open mode needs its own floor rule.
3. **Battery and data.** The radio cannot doze while open. Your own figures: about 180 mA while sending and 21 MB an hour. A 1000 mAh cell lasts hours, not days, so open mode is a dock feature.
4. **"Finished product" up front is a risk if it does not exist.** The images are generated concepts. A product-first hero must still say plainly it is a concept, or family will ask where to buy one.
5. **Vintage can hurt older eyes.** Sepia on beige, thin typewriter fonts, stencil body text and hover-only controls (C's parts photo) all fail the person the site is about. Keep contrast strong, body text large and nothing that only works on hover.
